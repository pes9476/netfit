- `POST /api/v1/auth/register/`, `login/`, `logout/`
- `GET/PATCH /api/v1/profile/me/`
- `GET/POST /api/v1/workouts/`, `GET /api/v1/workouts/summary/`
- `GET/POST /api/v1/groups/`, `POST /api/v1/groups/{id}/join/`
- `GET/POST /api/v1/missions/`
- `GET/POST /api/v1/battles/`, `POST /api/v1/battles/{id}/join/`
- `GET/POST /api/v1/proofs/`
